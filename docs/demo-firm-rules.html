PropCourt Demo Firm: Trading Rules

Demo Evaluation

These rules apply to every Demo Evaluation account.

Maximum Daily Loss

Your losses on any one trading day may not reach 5% of the initial account balance. Trading days run from 00:00 to 23:59 CET.

Maximum Loss

Your balance may never fall by 10% or more below the initial account balance.

Minimum Trading Days

You must trade on at least 3 trading days before requesting a payout.

Prohibited conduct

  • Hedging, holding opposite positions in the same instrument at the same time, is not allowed.
  • Positions may not be held over the weekend; close all trades before the Friday market close.

Payouts

The trader receives 80% of net closed profit. The firm reviews each request and may deny it only by citing one of the rules on this page.
